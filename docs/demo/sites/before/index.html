Welcome to our website!

Welcome to Larkspur Bakehouse. We are a small bakery on Mill Lane. All of our bread and pastries are baked fresh every morning in our shop.

You can pre-order bread for the next day using the form on this page. Please order before 6pm. Payment is taken when you collect your order.

About Us

Our bakers start at 4am. We feed the levain, mix the dough, shape the loaves by hand and bake them so they are ready when we open at 7am.
