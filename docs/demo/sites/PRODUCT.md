# Product: Larkspur Bakehouse (synthetic demo subject)

Assumption notice: this is a fictional bakery made up to demonstrate UI Workflow Skills in a README video. Every name, price and address below is invented. Answers came from the plugin owner's brief ("a neighbourhood bakery's landing and order page; the plain old site turns into a polished one"), and the rest is inferred and labelled as such.

## What it is
A neighbourhood sourdough and pastry bakery with one shop. It bakes every morning, sells over the counter, and takes pre-orders for pickup the next day.

## Audience (inferred)
People who live or work nearby and decide on their phone or laptop, in the evening or early morning, what to pick up tomorrow. They want three things: what's baked today, when the shop opens, and how to reserve a loaf before it sells out.

## The job of the site
Get a visitor to pre-order for pickup tomorrow (primary action), or come by the shop (secondary).

## Content (synthetic)
- Name: Larkspur Bakehouse. Address: 14 Mill Lane. Open Tue–Sun, 7:00–14:00.
- Today's bakes: Country sourdough £5.20; Butter croissant £2.60; Cinnamon bun £3.10; Rye & seeds £5.80; Flat white £3.20.
- Story: the bakers start the dough at 4 a.m.; the levain is fed twice a day; everything sold is baked that morning.
- Pre-order: reserve by 18:00, pick up from 7:00 next day.
- Photos: bread on a board, croissants, cinnamon buns, the baker's hands in flour, the counter, coffee with a croissant.

## Must stay (the "before" site's identity the redesign keeps)
The name, the menu and prices, the opening hours, the address, the pre-order flow.

## What would feel wrong
Stock-template sameness, fake reviews or awards, chain-bakery gloss, invented claims ("best in town").
